Based on [Nagu Sensei's tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBjh1Vb-n6g), I reinterpreted my work "Pipette Warrior" from three years ago.
<br><br>
I want the character to grow as well, transforming from "an intern who messes around in the lab when the supervisor is away" into "a pioneer breaking outdated structures."
